About us

RR Digital was started by someone who works in oil and gas field operations: on leases, along pipelines, and around the equipment crews deal with every day. We've seen what slows a job down. Maps that live on one person's laptop. KMZ files emailed back and forth until nobody knows which one is current. A line someone marked last month that the next crew can't find.

ROW Mapper came out of that work. It's built around how field crews actually operate: no cell service on location, a truck waiting, and a supervisor who needs the map today, not next week. When problems came up in the field, we stayed with them until they were solved and the job could move forward. We build and support our apps the same way.

We also make games, because a good app should be easy and enjoyable to use, whether it's for work or for fun.
