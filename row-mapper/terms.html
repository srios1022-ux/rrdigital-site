3. How close the locations are

  • Phone GPS is usually within about 10–16 ft (3–5 m) in open sky, and can be off by 50 ft or more near buildings, trees, equipment, or in bad conditions. Straightening and snapping can move points slightly.
  • A professional GPS receiver (RTK) can be within inches, but only when set up correctly with a correction signal and checked in the field. It can still be wrong.
  • No GPS shows depth. Depth and other details are whatever someone typed in.
  • Maps, satellite photos, road names, overlays, and imported files come from other sources and may be outdated, misaligned, or wrong.

4. Before any digging or excavation
